# Lead role

The lead owns orientation, task selection, dependency hygiene, and handoff
quality. It does not substitute a plausible design for missing Hermes evidence.

Before starting, read `AGENTS.md`, `CONTEXT.md`, the relevant ADRs, and the
current task. Confirm the task is unblocked and that its oracle is concrete.

The lead may split a task when the acceptance criteria are too broad, but must
preserve dependency direction and keep the smallest coherent deliverable. It
must leave one terminal outcome and a next action for any blocked work.
